# Claims walk — the workspace's texts (host-v0.9.0)

**Method.** Every entry of the catalogue (`apps/web/src/copy/actions.ts`, `connect.ts`, `glossary.ts`, `pages.ts`, `states.ts`, the `TOKEN_WARNING` of `apps/web/src/connect/commands.ts`), every article, exit code and runbook line of `apps/web/src/help/articles.ts`, the heading of `apps/web/src/help/KnownLimits.tsx`, and the error table `apps/web/src/errors/explain.ts` (the help article "Error messages, A to Z" is generated from it) was read against the product as built on branch `session-9` (head `6233002`, with the working tree's uncommitted edits of 22 Nov 2026: the new `local-store`, `local-issue`, `server-store` and `TOKEN_WARNING` texts are the ones walked). Numbers were checked against `packages/contracts/src/limits.ts` (the catalogue reads most of them from `LIMITS`; the hard-coded ones were compared by hand: 5 failures, 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 12 hours, 20, 365, 1000 entries, 10 minutes, 7 days, ten codes, six digits, 30 seconds all match). What a button does was checked against `apps/web/src/screens/`, the CLI against `apps/cli/src/` and the consumer runbooks, error texts against `packages/contracts/src/errors.ts` and where the API raises each code. Nothing was run: the evidence is the test that states the claim (file and test name), a runbook line, or the code that makes it true. Verdicts: **TRUE** (a test or runbook states it), **TRUE (code only)**, **CORRECT** (says more than the product does, or is wrong; the replacement is in "Corrections"; once applied, the row reads **CORRECTED** with its new wording), **NO CLAIM** (labels and navigation). Integration tests are under `tests/integration/`, browser tests under `tests/browser/`.
